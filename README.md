# MMK Hub 🇨🇲
> **Direct Single-Seller Commercial & Wholesale Platform for Cameroon**
> *(Douala, Yaoundé, Bafoussam, Bamenda, Buea, Limbe & Nationwide)*

MMK Hub is an owner-operated e-commerce platform designed exclusively for Cameroon. Unlike open multi-vendor marketplaces, MMK Hub is direct single-seller: the owner directly controls stock, product authenticity, logistics, and pricing.

---

## 🌟 Key Architecture & Capabilities

1. **Dual-Channel Model (Retail vs Wholesale B2B)**:
   - **🛒 Retail Mode**: Individual buyers, single-unit orders, fixed transparent retail pricing, local courier dispatch, instant checkout with MTN MoMo, Orange Money, or Cash on Delivery.
   - **🏢 Wholesale Mode**: Volume discount tiers (-15% to -28%), Minimum Order Quantities (MOQs), 50% deposit upfront via Mobile Money + 50% on delivery inspection, and formal OHADA proforma invoicing.
2. **Cameroon Payment Rails**:
   - **MTN Mobile Money** (`*126#` simulator & transaction authorization)
   - **Orange Money Cameroon** (`#150#` simulator & transaction authorization)
   - **Cash on Delivery (COD)** for retail
   - **50% Deposit Settlement** for commercial wholesale
3. **Services & Bundles**:
   - Technical appointments (e.g. Solar panel and inverter installation) bookable with date and time slots.
   - Turnkey commercial bundles (e.g. 200W Commercial Solar System + On-Site Installation).
4. **Logistics & City Delivery Rates**:
   - Douala (Local Courier - 1 500 FCFA)
   - Yaoundé (Express Shuttle - 2 000 FCFA)
   - Bafoussam, Bamenda, Buea, Limbe (Regional Agency - 3 500 FCFA)
   - Akwa Douala Depot Pickup (Free)
   - Free delivery on orders exceeding 100 000 FCFA
5. **Real-Time Tracking & Invoicing**:
   - 4-stage tracking stepper (`Received` -> `Processing` -> `Shipped` -> `Delivered`)
   - Downloadable / Printable formal OHADA Proforma Invoices with payment stamp.
   - Direct WhatsApp order synchronization (`wa.me`).
6. **Owner Admin Control Center (`admin.php`)**:
   - KPI metrics: Total gross sales, retail vs wholesale breakdown, pending wholesale applicants.
   - Fulfillment status manager (`Received`, `Processing`, `Shipped`, `Delivered`).
   - Wholesale balance settlement recorder.
   - 1-click wholesale business approval / rejection.
   - Product, stock, MOQ, and tier management.
   - Custom quotes inbox.

## 🚀 Getting Started Locally

1. Place this directory in `C:/xampp/htdocs/mmkhub.com`
2. Start Apache in **XAMPP Control Panel**
3. Open your browser and navigate to:
   - Storefront: `http://localhost/mmkhub.com/`
   - Owner Admin: `http://localhost/mmkhub.com/admin.php`
   - B2B Wholesale Portal: `http://localhost/mmkhub.com/wholesale.php`
   - Order Tracking & Invoices: `http://localhost/mmkhub.com/track.php`
